## Current thesis

AI servers and 800G→1.6T high-speed networking are pushing copper-clad laminate materials toward lower-loss, higher-grade generations such as M8/M9. The thesis is no longer only a specification-upgrade story: M9 shipments, pricing actions, high utilization/capacity expansion, and profit/EPS revisions are becoming visible. However, the net CCL value uplift under CPO/optical architectures still requires validation rather than linear extrapolation.

## Contradictory evidence

1. **CPO can change where value is captured.** If more high-speed signaling moves closer to optical engines/package-level interconnect, some motherboard CCL content could face substitution even as overall system bandwidth rises.
2. **Qualification is not shipment.** 聯茂 and 南亞 have advanced M9/M10 validation, but qualification should not be treated as locked commercial volume.
3. **Crowding is rising.** Breadth is 100% and heat is 1.24, so price can run ahead of earnings evidence.
4. **Raw-material tightness cuts both ways.** Tight copper foil, glass fabric and resin can support pricing, but incomplete pass-through would pressure margins.
5. **Capacity additions can normalize scarcity.** Large 2027 capacity increases need to be matched against actual customer reservations and platform ramps.

## Open questions

- Will M9 become a meaningful 2026H2 revenue contributor across the group, or is most volume still a 2027 event?
- Does CPO reduce motherboard CCL value in some architectures, and if so, is that offset by higher-layer/high-speed boards elsewhere in the rack?
- Can price increases continue to outpace raw-material costs?
- Are 2027 capacity additions genuinely backed by customer reservations and production schedules?
- Why is 台光電 price behavior diverging from other CCL names: valuation/crowding, product mix, or customer timing?

## What changed vs previous state

This is the first longitudinal CCL research baseline because CCL became an Active Theme in the 2026-10-02 GitHub state. The new second-layer conclusion is that the thesis has moved beyond a pure market narrative: M9 shipment, capacity expansion, price actions, margin improvement and a fresh 台燿 EPS revision are now observable. The most important counter-thesis is that CPO may redistribute rather than simply increase CCL value.

## Thesis assessment

**Thesis Strengthening**

The causal chain from specification upgrade to shipments, pricing, margins and EPS is increasingly supported. The thesis would move toward Intact/Weakening if M9 stays stuck in qualification, CPO materially reduces board-level CCL content, capacity comes online faster than demand, or EPS revisions stop keeping pace with price expectations.

台燿本批官方簡報的財務表格未完整抽取，採7/29公司公告轉載的半年數據；與另外三家的第二季單季不能直接排名。原底稿第二季EPS8.02等新聞來源仍保留，其股本口徑與部分資料庫不同，下一步須以官方財報原件再核。

### 研究結論與下一步

產業成長不是四家公司同一條路：高階材料、一般伺服器、成本轉嫁、垂直整合和轉投資必須拆開。官方實績可以驗證已發生的收入，但產品認證與規劃產能不足以證明2027訂單已鎖定。本次將訂單／出貨與產能確認度改為部分確認。
