## Current thesis

AI server rack power density continues to rise, pushing cooling architecture from air cooling toward direct liquid cooling, cold plates, manifolds and CDU-related components. Current evidence still supports demand and shipment growth, but the market theme has already entered a mature phase, so valuation/crowding and execution risk matter more than in the early cycle.

## Conflicting evidence / disconfirming checks

1. China and other competitors are expanding liquid-cooling capacity, which can pressure pricing and future margins.
2. Fast capacity additions can turn a shortage narrative into normalization if platform ramps are delayed.
3. Qualification timing for next-generation platforms can shift quarterly shipment timing.
4. Market recognition is already high; strong fundamentals do not automatically imply further valuation expansion.

## What changed vs previous state

This is the initial longitudinal baseline file. Relative to the registry/market state, the fundamental chain is stronger than a pure theme narrative: demand, capacity expansion, revenue and margins are already visible. The main new caution is that the theme is Mature rather than early-stage, so future updates should focus on whether earnings revisions continue to outrun already-high expectations.

## Thesis assessment

**Thesis Strengthening**

The operating evidence still points to stronger liquid-cooling penetration and supplier earnings, while EPS consensus is still being revised upward. The thesis would weaken if capacity grows faster than orders, qualification delays persist, ASP/margins roll over, or EPS revisions flatten/turn negative.
